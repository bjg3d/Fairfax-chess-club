Why people come back

A welcoming place to play serious chess without an intimidating atmosphere.

Most nights are simple: show up, get a game, talk through positions, and meet other local players who like helping each other improve.

Some players come for casual untimed games. Others want clock practice, opening discussion, or confidence before their first in-person tournament. The club makes room for all of that without making any one format the whole point.

Join

A simple way to turn online chess into real-board confidence.

  1. Pick Monday or Wednesday and check the Meetup page for the latest note before you come.
  2. If you mostly play online or it is your first club visit, say so. Someone can help you get oriented and find a good pairing.
  3. Play a few games, review interesting positions, and come back whenever you want more over-the-board reps.

FAQ

Questions players usually ask first.

Do I need to be good at chess?

No. The club is useful for beginners, intermediate players, casual players, and experienced players.

I only play online. Should I still come?

Yes. This is one of the best reasons to come. The club is a low-pressure place to get comfortable with real boards, clocks, and in-person games.

Do I need to bring equipment?

Bring a board and clock if you have them. If not, come anyway and someone can usually help you get into a game.

Are kids welcome?

Players of all ages are welcome. Parents or guardians should use their judgment and stay with minors unless they have confirmed another arrangement with an organizer.

Are games rated?

Most weekly games are casual. If a rated or tournament-style event is planned, it should be called out separately.

Is there a cost?

No formal dues are listed on this site. Since the club meets at restaurants, please support the venues by buying food or a drink when you can.
